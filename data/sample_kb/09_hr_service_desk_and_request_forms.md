# Enterprise Standard Operating Procedure: HR Service Desk & Request Workflows

**Document ID:** HR-SOP-009  
**Version:** 3.0  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Applicability:** Global Employees  

---

## 1. Overview of HR Support Channels
Employees can access HR services, request formal certificates, resolve payroll queries, or report grievances via three primary channels:
1. **Self-Service Portal:** `https://hr.enterprise.internal` (Automated generation of employment letters, payslips, and tax forms).
2. **HR Help Desk Ticketing:** JIRA Service Management queue (`People Support Desk`).
3. **Dedicated HRBP Connect:** Monthly 1-on-1 office hours with your department's designated HR Business Partner.

---

## 2. Standard Service Level Agreements (SLAs)

| Request Category | Examples | Target Resolution Time |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **P0 - Emergency / Critical** | Medical emergency, bereavement support, payroll non-receipt | **< 4 Business Hours** |
| **P1 - Standard Letters** | Employment verification, visa invitation letter, bonafide letter | **< 24 Business Hours** |
| **P2 - Policy Clarifications** | Leave calculation, benefits coverage, tuition assistance inquiry | **< 48 Business Hours** |
| **P3 - System Profile Updates** | Tax declaration update, bank details change, address change | **< 3 Business Days** |
| **P4 - Complex Grievance** | Mediation, compensation review, transfer requests | **< 10 Business Days** |

---

## 3. Standard HR Request Forms & Requisitions

### 3.1 Employment Verification & Visa Support Letters
- Automated generation via HRMS self-service for standard employment verification letters.
- For customized consulate or embassy visa sponsorship letters requiring official corporate seal and HR Director signature, submit Request Form **HR-REQ-012** at least **5 days** prior to visa appointment.

### 3.2 Internal Job Transfer (IJP) Requisition
- Employees who have completed at least **12 months in their current role** with a rating of "Meets Expectations" or higher are eligible to apply for internal lateral transfers or promotions.
- Requires initial notification to current reporting manager and submission of form **HR-REQ-034**.

### 3.3 Tuition & Professional Certification Reimbursement Claim
- Prior approval from manager and L&D team is required via form **HR-REQ-055** before course enrollment.
- Upon passing/completion, submit course invoice, payment receipt, and official certificate of passing within 30 days.
