# Enterprise Human Resources Policy: Leave and Time-Off Guidelines

**Document ID:** HR-POL-001  
**Version:** 3.2  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Applicability:** All Full-Time and Part-Time Employees  

---

## 1. Overview
This policy establishes the standard entitlements, approval workflows, and compliance guidelines governing employee time-off, holidays, and leaves of absence at the Enterprise.

## 2. Leave Categories and Annual Entitlements

### 2.1 Paid Annual Leave (Earned Leave)
- **Entitlement:** All full-time employees accrue **24 working days** of Paid Annual Leave per calendar year (accrued at the rate of 2.0 days per completed month of service).
- **Carry-over Rules:** A maximum of **10 unused annual leave days** can be carried forward into the next calendar year. Any excess days beyond 10 are encashed automatically on December 31 at the employee's base daily wage.
- **Notice Period:** Annual leaves lasting 3 consecutive days or more require submission of an HR portal request at least **7 business days** in advance.

### 2.2 Medical & Sick Leave
- **Entitlement:** Employees receive **12 days** of paid sick leave per year, credited on January 1.
- **Medical Documentation:** For any sick leave extending beyond **2 consecutive days**, a formal medical certificate signed by a licensed medical practitioner must be uploaded to the HR portal within 48 hours of return to work.
- **Carry-over:** Unused sick leaves lapse at the end of each calendar year and cannot be encashed or carried forward.

### 2.3 Parental & Family Leave
- **Maternity Leave:** Primary caregivers are entitled to **26 weeks (182 calendar days)** of fully paid maternity leave for up to two surviving children. Can be initiated up to 8 weeks prior to the expected delivery date.
- **Paternity Leave:** Secondary caregivers receive **4 weeks (28 calendar days)** of fully paid paternity leave, usable within the first 6 months following childbirth or legal adoption.
- **Adoption Leave:** Eligible parents legally adopting a child under the age of 1 year are entitled to **16 weeks** of paid leave.

### 2.4 Bereavement Leave
- Up to **5 consecutive business days** of fully paid bereavement leave are granted in the unfortunate event of the demise of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling, legal guardian).
- Up to **2 consecutive business days** for extended family (grandparent, parent-in-law).

### 2.5 Compensatory Off (Comp-Off)
- Employees who are required by their supervisor to work on a declared company holiday or designated rest day earn one compensatory day off.
- Comp-off must be approved by the Department Head and consumed within **60 days** of accrual.

---

## 3. Leave Application Workflow
1. Submit the leave request through the internal HR Portal (`https://hrportal.enterprise.internal/leaves`).
2. The direct manager receives an automated notification and must approve or decline within **48 hours**.
3. In case of medical emergencies, notify your team lead and HR Business Partner (HRBP) via email or phone on day one, followed by formal system logging upon return.

---

## 4. Unpaid Leave and Loss of Pay (LOP)
Any absence without approved leave balance or unauthorized absence exceeding 3 consecutive days will be marked as Loss of Pay (LOP) and may initiate disciplinary proceedings under the Attendance Code.
