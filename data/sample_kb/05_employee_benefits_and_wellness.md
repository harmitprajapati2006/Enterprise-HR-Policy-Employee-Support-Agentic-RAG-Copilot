# Enterprise Policy: Employee Benefits, Healthcare & Wellness Programs

**Document ID:** HR-POL-005  
**Version:** 3.0  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Applicability:** Full-Time Regular Employees & Dependents  

---

## 1. Group Medical & Health Insurance Coverage
- **Coverage Sum:** Comprehensive group medical and hospitalization insurance up to **$500,000 USD** (or local INR equivalent 10,00,000 INR per family floater).
- **Covered Beneficiaries:** Employee, lawful spouse/domestic partner, and up to 2 dependent children (up to age 25).
- **Pre-existing Conditions:** Covered from Day 1 of joining with cashless hospitalization across 8,000+ network hospitals.
- **Maternity In-Patient Coverage:** Standard natural delivery up to $4,000 / Caesarean section up to $6,000.
- **Dental & Vision Allowance:** Direct reimbursement up to **$500 USD per family member per year** for dental prophylaxis, corrective optical lenses, and routine eye exams.

## 2. Mental Health & Employee Assistance Program (EAP)
- The Enterprise partners with Modern Health to provide 24/7/365 confidential psychological support.
- Each employee and their adult dependents receive **12 free one-on-one sessions** per year with licensed clinical psychologists, psychiatrists, or accredited executive coaches.
- 100% confidential: No individualized health or utilization data is ever disclosed to managers or HR.

## 3. Fitness & Physical Wellness Stipend
- All employees are eligible for an annual **$600 USD Wellness Stipend** ($50/month) applicable towards gym memberships, yoga studios, marathon registrations, fitness trackers, or ergonomic sporting gear.

## 4. Continuing Professional Education (L&D Allowance)
- To encourage continuous learning, each employee is allocated **$1,500 USD annually** towards books, Coursera/Udemy enterprise subscriptions, industry conferences, and approved professional certifications (e.g. AWS, PMP, SHRM, GCP, Kubernetes).

## 5. Annual Preventive Health Check-Up
- Employees aged 30 and older are entitled to one complimentary full-body executive preventive health screening each calendar year at an empanelled diagnostics laboratory.
