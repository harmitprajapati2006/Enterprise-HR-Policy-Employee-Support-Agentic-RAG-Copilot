# Enterprise Policy: Remote, Hybrid, and Flexible Work Framework

**Document ID:** HR-POL-002  
**Version:** 2.4  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Applicability:** Global Workforce  

---

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this policy is to outline expectations, eligibility, equipment provisioning, and security standards for employees participating in hybrid or remote work arrangements.

## 2. Work Models

### 2.1 Hybrid Model (Default for Office-Assigned Roles)
- Employees are expected to work from an designated enterprise hub a minimum of **3 days per week**, with up to **2 days remote**.
- Tuesdays and Thursdays are designated as **Anchor Collaboration Days** where all hybrid team members are expected on-site for cross-functional alignment.

### 2.2 Fully Remote Model
- Full remote status requires formal approval from the Department VP and Chief People Officer (CPO).
- Eligibility is based on role requirements, past performance ratings (exceeding/meeting expectations), and residence location.

## 3. Core Working Hours and Availability
- Regardless of physical location, all employees must remain accessible and online during **Core Business Hours**: **10:00 AM to 4:00 PM (Local Regional Timezone)**.
- Employees must update their calendar status, Slack/Teams presence indicator, and respond to asynchronous direct messages within **2 hours** during business hours.

## 4. Equipment, Home Office Stipend & Connectivity
- **Company Hardware:** Enterprise IT provides an enterprise-managed laptop, secondary monitor (or requisition credit), and secure docking station.
- **One-time Home Setup Allowance:** Newly hired permanent employees receive a one-time, non-taxable stipend of **$800 USD** (or local currency equivalent) to furnish an ergonomic workstation.
- **Monthly Internet & Utility Allowance:** Remote and hybrid employees receive a monthly reimbursement of **$60 USD** towards broadband Internet connectivity through the expensing portal.

## 5. Security & Confidentiality Standards
- Work must only be conducted on enterprise-managed devices with active endpoint encryption, BitLocker, and CrowdStrike Falcon agents enabled.
- Connecting to unsecured public Wi-Fi networks (airports, cafes, hotels) without initiating the Enterprise GlobalProtect VPN is strictly prohibited.
- Family members or third parties are strictly forbidden from viewing or accessing company screens, devices, or intellectual property.

## 6. Work from Anywhere (Temporary International Remote Work)
- Employees in good standing may request up to **20 working days per calendar year** to work remotely from an approved international jurisdiction.
- The request must be logged 30 days in advance via HR Desk to review tax residency, cybersecurity compliance, and legal restrictions.
