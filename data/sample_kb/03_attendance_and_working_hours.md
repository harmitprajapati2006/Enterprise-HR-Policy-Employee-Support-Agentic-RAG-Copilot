# Enterprise Human Resources Policy: Attendance, Working Hours & Punctuality

**Document ID:** HR-POL-003  
**Version:** 2.1  
**Effective Date:** February 1, 2026  
**Applicability:** All Employees and Contract Personnel  

---

## 1. Standard Working Schedule
- **Official Work Week:** Monday through Friday.
- **Standard Working Hours:** 8 hours per day, totaling **40 hours per week**, exclusive of meal breaks.
- **Core Operating Windows:** Standard working shifts span between **09:00 AM and 06:00 PM** with a mandatory 1-hour lunch break and two optional 15-minute rest breaks.

## 2. Punctuality and Flexible Arrival Window
- The Enterprise operates a **30-minute flexi-window**. Employees may begin their workday between **08:30 AM and 09:30 AM**, completing their 8-hour shift between **05:30 PM and 06:30 PM**.
- Arrivals later than 09:30 AM must be communicated to the team supervisor via Slack/Teams prior to 09:00 AM.
- Three or more unexcused late arrivals within a single billing cycle will trigger an informal counseling session with the HR Business Partner.

## 3. Time Logging & Shift Rostering
- Non-exempt hourly employees must record arrival, lunch recess, and departure times via the electronic HRMS biometric reader or mobile app geofence check-in.
- For exempt/salaried staff, regular presence during core collaboration hours (10:00 AM - 4:00 PM) constitutes satisfactory attendance.

## 4. Overtime & Weekend Duty Guidelines
- Overtime must be pre-approved in writing by the direct Manager and Department Director.
- Eligible non-exempt employees working beyond 40 weekly hours will receive overtime pay computed at **1.5 times the regular hourly rate** (or 2.0 times for recognized national holidays).

## 5. Official Company Holidays
- The organization declares **11 statutory paid holidays** each year, published annually by People Operations on December 1.
- In addition, employees receive **2 Floating/Optional Holidays** to celebrate personal cultural, religious, or civic occasions of their choosing.
