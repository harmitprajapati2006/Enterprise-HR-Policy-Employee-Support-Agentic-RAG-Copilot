# Enterprise Policy: Payroll, Compensation & Expense Reimbursements

**Document ID:** HR-POL-004  
**Version:** 4.0  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Applicability:** Global Full-Time, Part-Time, and Intern Employees  

---

## 1. Payroll Cycle & Disbursement Dates
- **Disbursement Day:** Salaries are credited directly via Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT/Direct Deposit) on the **last business day of every calendar month**.
- If the last calendar day falls on a Saturday, Sunday, or declared banking holiday, salary disbursement occurs on the immediately preceding business day.
- **Pay Slips:** Itemized pay slips are generated automatically and available for download on the 1st of the following month via the HR Self-Service Portal (`https://payroll.enterprise.internal`).

## 2. Salary Components & Structure
Total Cost to Company (CTC) is structured into:
1. **Base Salary:** Fixed component paid monthly.
2. **Housing & Cost of Living Allowance:** Regional adjustment component.
3. **Provident Fund / 401(k) Contribution:** Company matches employee retirement contributions up to **6% of annual base salary**.
4. **Variable Performance Bonus:** Disbursed semi-annually (July and January) based on individual OKRs and corporate performance metrics.

## 3. Expense Reimbursement Policy
Employees incurring legitimate business expenses during corporate travel, client entertainment, or operational duties are eligible for prompt reimbursement:
- **Eligible Categories:** Airfare/train travel (economy class), lodging up to $220/night, meals up to $75/day per diem, client hospitality, and authorized certification exam fees.
- **Submission Deadline:** Expense claims with scanned receipts must be submitted via Concur/Expensify within **30 days** of expenditure.
- **Approval SLA:** Managers must review within 5 business days; finance processes disbursements in the subsequent bi-weekly pay run.

## 4. Salary Advance & Emergency Hardship Assistance
- Employees who have completed at least 6 months of continuous service can apply for an emergency salary advance equal to up to **1 month of base salary** in verified family or medical distress.
- Recovered across equal installments over a maximum repayment duration of 6 months, interest-free.
