# Enterprise Policy: Employee Personnel Records, Data Privacy & Confidentiality

**Document ID:** HR-POL-008  
**Version:** 2.0  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Applicability:** Global Personnel & HR Operations  

---

## 1. Scope & Privacy Commitments
The Enterprise complies with global privacy regulations (including GDPR, CCPA, and DPDP) to ensure fair, lawful, and secure processing of employee Personally Identifiable Information (PII).

## 2. Collected Employee Data Categories
- Identification Details: Full name, date of birth, national identity number, passport copy, tax ID.
- Contact Details: Residential address, personal phone number, designated emergency contacts.
- Financial & Banking Details: Bank account numbers, routing codes, tax withholding certificates.
- Performance & Career Records: Performance reviews, disciplinary notes, promotion letters, training certificates.

## 3. Storage, Access Controls & Security
- Employee data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit via TLS 1.3.
- Only authorized People Operations personnel, payroll administrators, and direct line managers (strictly for appraisal metrics) have access on a need-to-know basis.
- Direct managers cannot inspect confidential medical records, salary details of peers, or background check verification files.

## 4. Employee Right to Inspect & Correct Records
- Employees have the legal right to inspect their complete personnel dossier upon written request to People Operations.
- Inspection or data copy requests will be fulfilled within **5 business days**.
- Discrepancies in addresses, banking details, or emergency contacts can be updated immediately via the HR self-service portal.

## 5. Record Retention Period
- Resigned or terminated employee files are archived securely for **7 years** following the date of separation to fulfill statutory tax, labor law, and pension auditing requirements, after which digital records are permanently wiped.
