# Enterprise Policy: Code of Conduct, Workplace Ethics & Anti-Harassment

**Document ID:** HR-POL-007  
**Version:** 4.1  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Applicability:** Board Members, Officers, Full-Time Employees, Contractors  

---

## 1. Principles of Professional Conduct
The Enterprise upholds the highest standards of integrity, mutual respect, fairness, and accountability. All employees are expected to foster an inclusive, safe, and collaborative work atmosphere free from bias, retaliation, and harassment.

## 2. Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) & Anti-Discrimination
- **Zero-Tolerance Policy:** Sexual harassment, derogatory remarks, unwelcome sexual advances, hostile workplace behavior, or discrimination based on race, gender, sexual orientation, disability, age, or religion will result in immediate disciplinary action up to summary dismissal.
- **Internal Complaints Committee (ICC):** Employees may submit confidential complaints directly to the ICC via `ethics-committee@enterprise.internal` or through an anonymous incident hotline.
- **Investigation Timeline:** Every complaint is formally acknowledged within **24 hours**, and investigations are finalized within **30 business days**.
- **Retaliation Protection:** Retaliation against any employee reporting misconduct in good faith is illegal and strictly prohibited.

## 3. Conflict of Interest & Outside Employment (Moonlighting)
- Employees are strictly prohibited from engaging in external commercial employment, side businesses, advisory roles, or board positions that compete with the Enterprise or create conflict with working hours.
- Passive investments or teaching engagements require prior written disclosure and clearance from the Legal Compliance Officer.

## 4. Corporate Gifts and Entertainment Policy
- Modest promotional items (pens, notebooks, mugs) under **$50 USD** value may be accepted.
- Any gift, meal, or hospitality exceeding **$100 USD** must be declared in the Compliance Gift Register.
- Cash or cash-equivalent gifts (gift cards, crypto, vouchers) of any denomination are strictly forbidden.

## 5. Whistleblower Protection Hotline
Employees witnessing financial impropriety, data breaches, corruption, or regulatory non-compliance are urged to file a report via the 24/7 third-party Whistleblower Portal (`https://enterprise.ethicspoint.com`). Reports can be filed 100% anonymously.
