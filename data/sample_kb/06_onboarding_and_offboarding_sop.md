# Enterprise SOP: Employee Onboarding and Offboarding Lifecycle

**Document ID:** HR-SOP-006  
**Version:** 3.1  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Applicability:** People Operations, Hiring Managers, IT, and Employees  

---

## 1. Employee Onboarding Lifecycle

### 1.1 Pre-boarding (T-minus 14 to 3 Days)
- People Ops issues the offer letter and collects government ID, academic credentials, and previous employment verification.
- IT provisions corporate email credentials (`first.last@enterprise.com`), Okta SSO profiles, and dispatches the standard laptop hardware kit.

### 1.2 First Day & Week 1 (Orientation & Immersion)
- **Day 1:** People Operations orientation covering core values, culture, compliance training, benefits enrollment, and Buddy assignment.
- **Week 1:** Setup of development environments, access tokens, and introduction to team sprint cadence.

### 1.3 30-60-90 Day Milestones
- **Day 30:** First check-in with HRBP and manager to evaluate initial role alignment and onboarding satisfaction.
- **Day 60:** Mid-probation review of initial sprint deliverables and peer feedback.
- **Day 90:** Formal probation confirmation assessment. Successful completion transitions employee to confirmed status.

---

## 2. Employee Offboarding Lifecycle

### 2.1 Resignation & Notice Period Requirements
- Individual contributors in confirmed roles must provide a **30 calendar days notice period**.
- Senior Leaders (Director and above) and critical technical architects must provide a **60 to 90 calendar days notice period** as specified in their appointment contract.
- Notice period buyout may be permitted solely at company discretion and mutual written consent.

### 2.2 Knowledge Transfer (KT) & Handover Process
- The departing employee must document all ongoing projects, active repository links, credentials, and runbooks in the official Confluence handover template.
- Manager signs off on the KT Completion Certificate at least 3 business days before the final working day (LWD).

### 2.3 Asset Recovery and IT Deprovisioning
- All company assets (laptop, monitors, security key fobs, corporate credit cards) must be surrendered to IT Asset Management by 05:00 PM on the Last Working Day.
- Okta, Google Workspace, Slack, and GitHub accesses are automatically deprovisioned at 06:00 PM on the Last Working Day.

### 2.4 Final Settlement & Relieving Documentation
- Full and Final Settlement (FNF) comprising unpaid salary, accrued leave encashment, and deductions is processed within **30 business days** of the last working day.
- Relieving Letter and Experience Certificate are emailed automatically upon FNF clearance.
